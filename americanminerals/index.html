How we work

Mine to manufacturer.

A deposit becomes supply only after five stages, and most projects stall at one of them. We run all five under one company so nothing gets stranded halfway.

  1. Explore

    Field mapping, geophysics and core drilling on federal, state and private land.

  2. Permit

    Baseline water and habitat studies, tribal consultation and agreements with the towns nearby.

  3. Mine

    Small footprints, modern equipment, reclamation bonded before the first blast.

  4. Refine

    Concentrate to oxide to metal, in plants built in the United States.

  5. Deliver

    Long-term offtake with American makers of magnets, chips, batteries and defense systems.

Principles

How we mean to do it.

Under U.S. law

Every tonne is mined under American environmental, labor and safety rules. That costs more, and we price it in.

Good neighbors

We hire locally, publish our water data, and leave land in better shape than we found it.

Built to last

Deposits take decades to develop. We plan and finance on that timescale, not on the commodity cycle.
