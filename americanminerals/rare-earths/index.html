Neodymium & rare earths

Element 60 · Nd

Rare earths

Seventeen elements that are chemically almost identical and very hard to separate. Neodymium, praseodymium, dysprosium and terbium make the magnets in motors, turbines, drones and guided munitions.

What it's for

Uses

  • Permanent magnetsNeodymium–iron–boron magnets in EV motors, wind turbines, hard drives and speakers.
  • DefenseGuidance systems, actuators and radar. An F-35 contains about 920 pounds of rare earth materials (CRS).
  • CatalystsCerium and lanthanum in catalytic converters and petroleum refining.
  • Optics & phosphorsGlass polishing, lasers and display phosphors.

Pressure points

  1. 1965–1985Mountain Pass, California is the world's largest source of rare earths.
  2. 2010China cuts export quotas and rare earth prices spike.
  3. Apr 2025China requires export licenses for seven medium and heavy rare earths and the magnets made from them.
  4. Oct 2025China announces wider controls, then suspends them for a year after the November trade truce.

Our approach

The mine is the easy part. Separation takes hundreds of solvent-extraction stages, and metal-making and magnet-making come after that. We plan the whole chain, from ore to oxide to metal.
