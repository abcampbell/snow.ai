What US industry swore it
could not substitute.

During the Section 301 tariff rounds, thousands of US firms filed sworn product-exclusion requests with USTR — each one a statement of "we cannot substitute this input, here is why, here is the switching timeline." That is short-run substitution-elasticity microdata: the exact thing the literature hand-waves with long-run numbers. This extracts every filing into structured elasticity rows, and uses USTR's grant/deny decision as a quasi-label that validates the sworn claim.
