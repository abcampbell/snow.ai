METHOD

Gauge = per-document composite of (a) the S1 hawk/dove lexicon and (b) the two-axis topic lexicon (inflation±/growth± counted against each bank's own expanding baseline), each z-scored within bank, averaged, monthly-meaned, EWM-smoothed (3-month half-life), then z-scored. All lexicons run on NATIVE-language text — 15 languages, no translation. Rates are the country short-rate series ([cty].sr, Rose). Lead–lag uses monthly data, minimum 60 overlapping months per lag.

Full-sample z-scores and full-sample lexicon baselines make this a descriptive gauge, not a tradable signal — the walk-forward version of these signals earned pooled IC +0.23 (pre-registered baseline for the LLM scoring pass). Russia's rate series ends Feb-2022, Chile 2019, Colombia 2019 — their panels stop there.
