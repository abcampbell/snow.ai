Red Flags Alleged

  • Air Chateau MoU for 100 Midnights valued at up to $500M is implausible; operator has only one helicopter purchased November 2023 and has ceased operations
  • KakaoMobility 50-aircraft commitment fell apart after Archer failed to deliver for Q4 2024 demo in Goheung, Korea, yet order remains in backlog
  • Future Flight Global 'up to 116' Midnight order is tied to a shell company with no operational track record
  • U.S. Air Force 'up to $148M' Agility Prime contract has only awarded $33M with just $744,796 disbursed; $110M firm-fixed-price portion delivered only $1.3M
  • UAE Launch Edition was staged hover at photo-op location using obsolete aircraft recycled for marketing
  • Archer claimed to have delivered first Midnight aircraft to UAE but remains sole owner of aircraft supposedly delivered two times
  • Site visits to Covington, Georgia manufacturing facility in June, July and August 2025 showed little to no production activity despite claims of current production
  • Midnight M001 SN001 is pre-production model that will not be flown for credit toward FAA type certification
  • Company downgraded from building 6+ conforming aircraft to 3 conforming aircraft in progress, 6 planned, then to non-conformal aircraft
  • Flight testing target delayed from early 2024 to mid-2024 and still not commenced over 12 months later on conformal aircraft
  • Archer at only 15% Stage 4 Type Certification progress compared to Joby at 70%, indicating at least 2-year lag
  • Midnight aft lifter propellers changed from 2-bladed to 4-bladed design, invalidating all previous testing
  • Videos of Midnight deliberately muted to conceal excessive noise levels; third-party measurements show 80-90 dBA in hover versus Joby 65 dBA
  • Archer uses misleading fragmented demos: N302AX for VTOL, N703AX for CTOL, never demonstrated together on single prototype
  • Company digitally altered test flight clips and reused them to portray tests that did not happen
