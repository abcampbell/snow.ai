Red Flags Alleged

  • Multiple children hospitalized for potential heart failure and pulmonary edema within weeks of commercial launch
  • Data fabrication in HbA1c charts - two different versions of same dataset published with conflicting values
  • Withdrawal study heavily skewed to one site (Jennifer Miller) with 20-30 of 77 patients; ex-employees allege investigator was 'too much in bed with the company'
  • Pressure from Soleno management to downplay serious adverse events including hospitalizations for fluid overload and potential heart failure during trials
  • Two patients hospitalized for potential heart failure during Phase 3 trial not properly disclosed; events attributed to other factors despite no underlying cardiac/renal disease
  • Withdrawal study remains unpublished despite being basis of FDA approval
  • Trial functionally unblinded due to obvious side effects (rapid hair growth, edema) allowing placebo identification
  • Mechanistic fraud: diazoxide cannot cross blood-brain barrier, invalidating claimed CNS mechanism for hyperphagia reduction
  • Drug mechanistically increases hunger by suppressing insulin >50%, opposite of claimed effect
  • DEXA scans misrepresent 16 lbs of fluid retention over 3 years as lean body mass gains
  • Diabetic ketoacidosis case in trials inadequately disclosed in manuscripts despite prominent FDA label warnings
  • Artificial enrollment via parents inflating baseline hyperphagia scores to qualify for trials
  • Subjective endpoint (HQ-CT caregiver questionnaire) with 30% variance depending on which parent completes it
  • Suspect data showing implausibly sharp separation in withdrawal study HQ-CT scores in final weeks
  • Ex-employees allege specific investigator violated entry criteria and lacked academic objectivity
  • Co-authors not informed papers were being prepared or published; critical investigators allegedly removed as authors
