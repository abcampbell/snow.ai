Thesis
Bayer is undervalued at 11x 2017 earnings regardless of Monsanto acquisition outcome. If deal breaks, stock recovers to pre-announcement 14x multiple. If deal closes, combined entity trades at attractive 10x with double-digit EPS growth from synergies and portfolio strength.

Catalysts

  • Monsanto acquisition closing by end of 2017
  • Alternative outcome: deal break-up leading to stock recovery to pre-announcement 14x multiple
  • Covestro divestiture completion
  • Xarelto and Eylea reaching peak sales above previous guidance
  • Synergies realization of $1.5bn within 3 years post-close
  • New pharma pipeline drugs coming online prior to patent expirations

Key Risks Acknowledged

  • Regulatory approval uncertainty - market pricing Monsanto at $102 vs $128 deal price indicates skepticism
  • Product overlap requiring divestitures of up to $1.6bn in cottonseed and herbicide sales
  • Potential regulatory confusion between acquiring existing monopoly vs creating anti-competitive merger
  • Agricultural industry consolidation scrutiny from Senate Judiciary Committee
  • Equity rights offering creating perceived stock overhang
  • European opposition to GMOs and Monsanto's Agent Orange reputation
