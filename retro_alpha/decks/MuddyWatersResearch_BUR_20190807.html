Thesis
BUR egregiously misrepresents ROIC and IRRs through manipulated metrics and aggressive mark-to-model fair value gains comprising 53.9% of core litigation assets. Just four cases generated ~66% of net realized gains since 2012; remaining concluded investments generated only ~19% ROIC. Company is arguably insolvent with high liquidity risk.

  • CFO is wife of founder/CEO with history of four prior CFO/senior finance turnovers
  • Egregiously manipulating ROIC and IRR through seven identified techniques including categorizing losses as wins and counting uncertain/unlikely recoveries as cash returns
  • Actively misleading investors by conflating Realized Gains and Net Realized Gains to obscure aggressive fair value accounting

  • Pattern of monetizing Petersen claim just before period-ends to enable fair value markups
