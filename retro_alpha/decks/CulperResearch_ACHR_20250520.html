Red Flags Alleged

  • June 2024 transition flight violated FAA airworthiness criteria by adding propeller blades and keeping aft rotors powered instead of shut down and faired as required
  • Transition flight staged to unlock $55 million Stellantis funding tranche announced three weeks later
  • June 21, 2024 patent amendment disclosed 'potentially catastrophic' vibration issues causing potential 'bending failure or other type of damage to the aircraft'
  • Claimed aircraft 'fully assembled' and 'finishing up ground tests' February 27, 2025 but internal emails show flight test team did not receive aircraft until early March
  • April 10, 2025 fire department photos showed aircraft in state of disrepair with exposed internals, open panels, wooden blocks taped to fuselage; photos later deleted
  • April 28, 2025 test team emails state high-speed ground runs not yet begun, expected 'within the next several weeks' despite claims two months earlier that ground testing was 'finishing up'
  • May 12, 2025 claimed piloted flights would begin 'within days' but investigators saw only single low-speed ground run over 8 days, aircraft never left ground
  • Extensive undisclosed redesign evident in Q1 2025 photos showing changes to fuselage, landing gear, wings, boom arms, tilt rotors, propellers, and aft rotors despite repeated claims design needed 'no modifications' and was 'mature'
  • Claimed building six 'fully conforming' aircraft throughout 2024 but produced only single partially conforming aircraft
  • To date has never shown video of Midnight completing transition with aft rotors faired per FAA requirements despite claims of regular transition flights throughout 2024
  • April 2025 interview suggests aircraft no longer uses gearboxes, departing from historical claims of 'patented gearbox' design
  • Pulled out of Japan World Expo demonstration flight commitment citing 'timing issues' without disclosing to investors
  • Paid estimated $25-30 million for 2028 Olympics sponsorship and approximately $600,000 plus expenses for Jimmy Fallon appearance and Tonight Show promotion
  • FAA compliance verification documents progressed only from 12% to 15% over two quarters
  • February 2025 10-K disclosed for first time company may be unable to obtain FAA certification 'at all'
