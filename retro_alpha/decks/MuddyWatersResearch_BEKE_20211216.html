Red Flags Alleged

  • GTV inflated by ~65% in 2Q-3Q 2021; new home sales GTV inflated by ~126%
  • Commission revenues inflated by ~77-96% based on lower actual commission rates than reported
  • Ghost stores: brokerages listed as active on platform but shut down or nonexistent when visited
  • Clone stores: multiple stores on platform with same/similar location but only one exists physically
  • Store count inflated by at least 23%; field work in Langfang showed 59% overstatement
  • Agent count inflated by 26% on platform; SAIC data shows 106% inflation in Shanghai, 90% in Beijing
  • Sham acquisition of Zhonghuan routed through straw buyer controlled by likely company proxy
  • Round-tripping cash through Deyou 'pilot store program' to inflate commission revenues
  • Undisclosed Lianjia franchise operations improperly booked as company-owned store revenue
  • Platform contains fake agents including former employees who left the company
  • Inconsistent employee count disclosures between SEC filings and SAIC records
