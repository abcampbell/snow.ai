Red Flags Alleged

  • Battery management system technology purchased from Gentherm for less than $1 million (under $0.01 per share)
  • 30% fuel efficiency claim unsupported by scientific evidence and described as 'not even possible on a pure Physics level' by former BMS manager
  • PAM Transportation abandoned Hybrid-X after two years because savings were 'only a small %', not the claimed 30%
  • Minimal follow-on orders from pilot programs despite claims of 50+ fleet partnerships
  • Only 7 Hybrid-X systems sold to customers (2 to Wegmans, 3 to Penske, 2 to Eagle Transport) despite being listed as having 7+ customers
  • Dana invested in Hyliion but developed its own proprietary electric truck solution instead of using Hyliion technology
  • Same 30% fuel efficiency claim maintained across drastically different product designs (trailer-based 2015-2016, tractor-based 2017+)
  • Falsely claimed NASA credential - award was from Tech Briefs Media Group publisher, not NASA
  • Listed competition sponsors (DOE, Shell, Autodesk) as 'partners' on website
  • Zero Hyliion patents support Hypertruck ERX natural gas technology; all 12 patents relate to kinetic energy capture in trailer/tractor systems
  • False warranty claim - CEO stated Hybrid-X retrofit 'does not affect the warranty of the truck' but PACCAR told Gentherm 'there is no way in hell' they would allow unauthorized retrofits
  • Exaggerated pilot program claims including '30+ fleets in pilot program including McDonalds' and '50 fleets' without evidence of deployments
  • Former Gentherm manager stated Healy 'grossly exaggerated demand' and forecasts kept shrinking, straining relationships
