Red Flags Alleged

  • Fuel cost assumption of $7,000/kg HALEU is lowballed by a factor of 5x according to multiple industry experts; realistic cost is $30,000-$40,000/kg
  • Oklo's combined license application was denied by NRC in 2022 in unprecedented fashion described as worst 'flame out' a former commissioner had seen
  • Management has zero experience building or operating commercial nuclear power plants
  • No demonstration reactor or prototype built before pursuing commercial license, contrary to industry best practices
  • Only 88 employees compared to 450+ at competitors who won Big Tech deals
  • Non-binding LOIs in customer pipeline contain no actual commitments and lack finalized PPAs
  • Illustrative unit economics used by Street analysts without adjustment despite unrealistic fuel cost assumptions
  • Promoted overnight capital costs of ~$2,300-$4,600/kW vastly understate likely costs per experts
  • Sodium-cooled reactor design has well-documented history of leaks, fires, and reliability problems globally
