Catalysts

  • Microsoft declined $12 billion expansion option and signed larger deal with Nebius competitor
  • OpenAI shift from external compute purchases to operating own facilities under Stargate JV
  • AI workload transition from training to inference favoring hyperscalers with custom silicon
  • Rapid GPU obsolescence and price collapse (H100 rental prices down 88% from peak)
  • Expiration of IPO lock-up triggering insider selling
  • Cash burn requiring continuous capital markets access while leverage peaks at 6x

Red Flags Alleged

  • Customer concentration of 71% revenue from Microsoft in 2Q25, with Microsoft declining expansion and awarding contract to Nebius
  • Unit economics showing equity holders receive no cash flow during contract terms under realistic assumptions
  • Illustrative contract disclosure uses 'utopian' 80% EBITDA margins and assumes 75% residual revenue from 5-year-old chips
  • ROIC of only 2-5% well below cost of capital, destroying shareholder value
  • CoreWeave unwilling to include collar in all-stock Core Scientific acquisition, signaling lack of confidence in own valuation
  • Aggressive 6-year useful life for GPUs versus 4-year industry standard, flattering financials
  • 3Q adjusted operating income guidance ~$200m below interest expense of $350-390m
  • Backlog ex-OpenAI barely grew q/q in 2Q25
  • Revenue shortfall of ~$8 billion versus backlog coverage through 2Q27
