Thesis
Starboard Value argues Huntsman Corporation possesses attractive assets but has chronically underperformed due to poor execution, repeated broken promises to shareholders across three investor days (2014, 2016, 2018), lagging profitability versus peers (500-900bps EBITDA margin deficit), and inadequate Board oversight characterized by conflicts of interest and lack of accountability.

Catalysts

  • Election of independent directors with chemical operations expertise to improve Board accountability
  • Execution on sale process for non-core Textile Effects segment
  • Operational improvements in commercial execution, manufacturing, supply chain and operating expenses
  • Capital allocation discipline including $1 billion share repurchase commitment over two years

Red Flags Alleged

  • Board set management bonus targets consistently below investor day commitments (2014, 2016, 2018)
  • Board reduced 2020 compensation targets by 41% mid-year during COVID, unlike all proxy peers
  • Multiple undisclosed director interlocks and relationships creating lack of true independence
  • Monetized Venator stake for only $140 million versus $1+ billion commitment
  • Shortened nomination window from ~30 days to 10 days to disadvantage shareholders
  • Failed to deliver TCFD-aligned climate disclosures despite two years of shareholder requests and public commitments
