Thesis
DraftKings faces significant disruption from federally-regulated prediction exchanges (Kalshi, Polymarket) operating in sports betting with structurally better odds (~1.5% fee vs. 8-10% vig). Kalshi already captured ~30% of weekly NFL handle and ~79% of NCAA football handle vs. 2024 OSB levels, threatening DKNG's market share and revenue estimates through at least 2026 while litigation drags on.

Catalysts

  • Kalshi and Polymarket continue operating while litigation drags on (potentially through 2027 SCOTUS decision)
  • CFTC implements new federal rule clarifying jurisdiction over sports prediction exchanges (could take 1-2 years to resolve state challenges)
  • Potential Trump administration support via Congress amendment to CEA codifying federal jurisdiction
  • Q3 and Q4 2025 peak earnings season (MLB playoffs, NBA season start, NFL/NCAA football) showing market share loss
  • Kalshi parlay product launch on Sept 29, 2025 threatens DKNG's most lucrative revenue stream
  • Analyst downgrades as 2025 and 2026 revenue estimates prove too aggressive

Red Flags Alleged

  • Sell-side analysts using 'emotionally charged language' and downplaying competitive threat with terms like 'potentially challenging'
  • Market misinterpreting Kalshi handle calculation (incorrectly dividing by two)
  • Analysts miscalculating Kalshi hold rate as ~4% when research indicates ~1.5%
  • Consensus 2026 revenue estimates barely declining despite clear competitive threat only 3 months from year-end
  • DKNG NY handle declined 8.9% Week 3 to Week 4 while Kalshi NFL/NCAA handle surged 31% same period
  • Average monthly unique player growth slowed from 57% YoY in Q3'24 to just 6% YoY in Q2'25
  • Handle growth decelerated from ~15% YoY in Q4'24/Q1'25 to just 6.3% YoY in Q2'25

Key Risks Acknowledged

  • Uncertainty around final legal outcome - whether event contracts are federal derivatives or state-regulated gambling
  • Possible state victories forcing shutdown of prediction exchanges
  • OSB operators could eventually obtain gaming licenses for prediction markets in all 50 states
  • TAM expansion opportunity if prediction exchanges ultimately allowed in all states
