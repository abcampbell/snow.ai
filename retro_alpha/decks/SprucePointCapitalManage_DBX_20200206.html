Catalysts

  • Near-term churn acceleration from Q3 2019 price hikes and platform changes
  • Rising customer acquisition costs as registered user growth saturates
  • Increased capital lease repayments in 2020-2021 as 3-5 year equipment leases expire
  • Failure of 'New Dropbox' smart workspace pivot to gain enterprise traction
  • Key executive departures including CCO and CTOs undermining transformation

Red Flags Alleged

  • Misleading FCF presentation excludes capital lease repayments (deferred capex) and stock compensation cash costs, overstating margins by 2x
  • Churn obfuscation: only one mention in S-1, management claims stable mid-teens but refuses quarterly disclosure despite price hikes
  • Aggressive 5-year amortization of sales commissions despite zero remaining useful life of developed technology per 2018 10-K
  • Patent accounting concerns: 150 new patents added post-IPO but book value unchanged at $13m since 2016, developed technology fully amortized
  • Failed to accrue $48.5m cash liability for HelloSign acquisition compensation agreements, overstating book value
  • Fractured Samsung partnership unwinding as of October 2019 without analyst disclosure
  • CEO Houston appointed to Facebook board raises conflict and time commitment concerns during critical transformation period
  • Former revenue analyst Glassdoor review alleges poor data quality, broken systems, no single source of truth for KPIs, mass departures
  • Purged 100m (20%) of registered user accounts in 2016 as worthless long-inactive users
