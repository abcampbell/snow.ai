Red Flags Alleged

  • Up to 70% of revenues fabricated through fake student enrollments
  • Systematic overstatement of revenue via tracking of >20% of Q1 2020 classes showing 60% discrepancy
  • Duplicate classes counted multiple times to inflate elementary school program revenues
  • Fake student users posting identical positive comments in WeChat groups
  • Approximately 80% of user accounts fabricated according to analysis of course comments
  • Company absent from all major Chinese government and industry surveys despite claimed hyper-growth
  • 75% discrepancy between PRC credit reports and SEC filings for net profit that management cannot explain
  • Related party transactions with undisclosed entities (Beijing Youlian) used to remove expenses
  • Star teachers claimed as growth driver are never named, not under contract, not listed anywhere
  • Geographic data shows ~50% of Q1 2020 students from Wuhan area receiving free classes, contradicting paid revenue claims
  • Company claims students from lower-tier cities but data shows mainly Tier 1 and 2 cities
  • Management deflecting criticism with vague non-quantitative answers
