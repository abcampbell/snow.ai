Thesis
Athenahealth is a bubble stock trading at 4x fair value. Despite missing revenue and margin targets, shares quintupled. Morgan Stanley's $192 DCF uses implausible assumptions (30% margins, $63k revenue/doctor). Realistic assumptions yield $50 value. Epic dominates hospital IT; capitation threatens athena's collection business model.

Catalysts

  • Consolidation of physician practices by hospitals forcing migration to Epic
  • Loss of six identified major customers
  • End of EHR government subsidies reducing new customer acquisition
  • Increased competition from lower-priced competitors (kareo, eClinicalWorks, CareCloud)
  • Shift to capitation payment models eliminating need for collections business

Red Flags Alleged

  • CEO promotional behavior disconnected from fundamentals
  • Missed organic revenue growth targets for 2013
  • Operating margins declining versus prior guidance of margin improvement
  • Misclassification as cloud/SaaS company when actually a BPO business
  • CVS MinuteClinic chose Epic over athena for EHR despite being longtime RCM client
  • OIG reversed anti-kickback exemption ending health information exchange business model
  • CEO admits pricing at negative margins for new Enterprise Coordinator product
  • Half-baked hospital solution years behind competitors
  • Capital misallocation risk from CEO using profits as 'huge can of fix it'
