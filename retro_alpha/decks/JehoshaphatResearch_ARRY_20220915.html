Catalysts

  • FY23 gross margin miss as Street expects 20% but true margins are 10-11%
  • Debt covenant violations as EBITDA collapses (6x leverage becomes 11x with realistic margins)
  • Continued Blackstone stock sales (Form 144 filed for 3.0m additional shares)
  • Receivables collection failures as DSOs continue exploding (up 100%+ over two years)
  • Product obsolescence as competitors launch new products while ARRY has no new product since 2015

Red Flags Alleged

  • Bill-and-hold revenue recognition inflating revenues by ~$300m aggregate
  • Recognizing revenue before product manufactured per former FP&A employee: 'we would recognize revenue as soon as we put in an order'
  • Unbilled DSOs exploding from 5 days to 35+ days in two years
  • Total DSOs up from ~40 days to ~90 days (>100% increase)
  • STI stopped collecting receivables before acquisition to inflate ARRY's post-acquisition cash flow by ~$92m
  • STI overstated gross margins at 30% by manufacturing excess inventory under Spanish GAAP (real margin 15%)
  • Former employee: 'We had to do it to make our loan covenants'
  • Gross margins inflated by selling only high-margin components first in Safe Harbor years
  • Raw material inventory days surging to ~90 from ~70, portending future COGS pressure not yet in P&L
  • Exodus of CEO, COO, Chief Commercial Officer, General Counsel, CTO, Chief Procurement Officer in 18 months
  • Former employee: '90-100% of the company's manpower has turned over in the last five years'
  • Minimal R&D spending (no R&D line item in income statement)
  • No new products since DuraTrack v3 launched in 2015 (7 years)
  • Former employee: 'Array isn't investing in new tech, they're stamping out product until they die, and obsolescence is on its way'
