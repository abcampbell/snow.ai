Catalysts

  • New schools and enrollment cap expansion (10 new schools and 17,000 new seats mentioned)
  • Increased regulatory scrutiny from states and IRS
  • Knowledge Universe distributed entire stake to investors (September 2013)
  • Deteriorating academic results triggering regulatory action

Red Flags Alleged

  • Aggressive student recruitment targeting at-risk students least likely to succeed in online schools
  • Enrollment violations, uncertified teachers, illegally siphoning profits from nonprofit entities
  • Misleading sales tactics with high-pressure call centers using fabricated statistics
  • Scantron test results unreliable: given unsupervised at home, only 52% completion rate, self-selected sample
  • Only 28% of K12 schools made Adequate Yearly Progress in 2010-11 vs 52% nationally
  • Aggressive capitalization of software and curriculum development costs (boosted EBITDA 21% in FY2013)
  • Uses BDO USA rather than Big Four accounting firm
  • Charging states for students who no longer attend or never showed up
  • Teachers told to pass every student regardless of performance
  • Days Sales Outstanding doubled in five years (impacting cash flows)
  • Enrollment cap violations and accepting students without required documentation
  • Targeting dropout and special education students who demand least education
  • Growth-at-any-cost culture prioritizing shareholder returns over student outcomes
  • Marketing spending of $340 per student (5.2% of per-pupil expenditures), including on Nickelodeon and teen social networks
  • Knowledge Universe (founder) distributed entire stake in September 2013
  • Steady insider selling since IPO

Key Risks Acknowledged

  • Shorting is described as 'horrible business' that cost the fund significantly over 4.5 years
  • No 'foreboding feeling that the world will fall apart' like in 2007, making shorting harder
  • Strong political support, especially among Republicans, for school choice
  • Online learning has enormous buzz (MOOCs, etc.)
  • High short interest (13.7% of shares) down from 27.2% at year start
