Catalysts

  • New CEO Robin Hayes implementation of ROIC-focused initiatives announced at November analyst day
  • Implementation of overbooking (100-200bps load factor improvement expected)
  • Cabin refresh adding 15 seats per A320 aircraft beginning mid-2016
  • Fare Families and first-checked-bag fees rollout
  • Acceleration of code-sharing agreements following pilot unionization
  • Falling oil prices providing tailwind to profitability

Key Risks Acknowledged

  • Airlines have historically destroyed capital; sustainability of above-average ROICs uncertain
  • JBLU may find it difficult to balance three stakeholders (customers, employees, shareholders)
  • Pilot unionization may be followed by other employee groups, potentially impacting service levels
  • Bag fees could impact demand
  • Recession scenario could cause 4% pricing drop and 5% load factor decline
  • JBLU's cost advantage will erode as fleet and pilot workforce ages
