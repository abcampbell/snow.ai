Catalysts

  • Board reconstitution with retail sector expertise and ownership perspectives
  • Transparent strategic alternatives review process with credible acquirers
  • Potential sale-leaseback of real estate assets (~$4 billion opportunity)
  • Larger share repurchase programs
  • Improved merchandising execution and direct sourcing
  • Zero-based cost budgeting and SG&A optimization

Red Flags Alleged

  • Board rejected credible, well-capitalized acquirer indications without sufficient access/information
  • Implemented two-tiered poison pill designed to chill acquirer interest
  • Opaque strategic alternatives review process timed to protect incumbent directors
  • Misaligned executive compensation rewarding executives for declining results
  • Management repeatedly overpromised and underdelivered on strategic plans since 2014
  • Refusal to adopt universal proxy card
  • Poor governance including lack of executive diversity and entrenched board
  • Process appears designed for 'optics sake' while retaining Board control through AGM

Key Risks Acknowledged

  • Execution risk in turnaround given decade of underperformance
  • Macroeconomic environment evolution affecting potential acquirers
  • Competitive retail environment and continued market share pressure
  • Capital intensive plan requiring sales growth that has not materialized historically
