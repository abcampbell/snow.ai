Thesis
Taubman Centers requires board and governance changes to address persistent underperformance, dual-class structure disenfranchisement, and capital allocation failures. Nominate shareholder representative Jonathan Litt, remove COO Billy Taubman from board, and eliminate dual-class voting structure to align interests with common shareholders.
