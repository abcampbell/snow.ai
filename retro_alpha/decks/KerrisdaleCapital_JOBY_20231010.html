Red Flags Alleged

  • Misleading battery specifications claiming 100-mile range and 10,000 cycle life when physics limits actual range to 25-35 miles
  • Impossibly low $1.3M unit cost projection when comparable aircraft cost $2M+ and competitors project $3-4M
  • Deceptive comparison claiming 1/4th helicopter operating costs using incomparable 16-seat dual-engine helicopter as basis
  • Misleading certification timeline presenting 60% completion when substantive testing stages 4-5 have barely begun
  • Unrealistic production projections of thousands annually with no aerospace manufacturing experience
  • Business model assuming 36 flights per day per aircraft 365 days/year despite daytime-only VFR-only certification limits
  • Unaddressed thermal runaway fire risk from high-power use of automotive battery cells not optimized for aviation
  • Battery fire certification path claimed as clear when FAA has not yet responded to proposals
