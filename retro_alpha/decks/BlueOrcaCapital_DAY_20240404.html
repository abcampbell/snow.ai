Red Flags Alleged

  • Inappropriate revenue pull forward through manipulation of stand-alone selling prices for professional services inflates revenues and profits by estimated $104 million
  • Auditor rescinded clean opinion mid-year and issued adverse opinion on internal controls specifically related to revenue recognition and professional services allocation
  • Growing balance of contract assets (unbilled revenue) doubled from $43M in 2019 to $89M in 2023, suggesting aggressive revenue recognition
  • Professional services revenue is 16-19% of total ex-float revenue vs peers at 2-10%, despite outsourcing 50%+ of implementations to third parties
  • Misleading non-GAAP metric 'adjusted cloud recurring gross margin' arbitrarily excludes $98M product management costs and $67M depreciation/amortization while including float revenue
  • Doubled commission amortization period from 5 to 10 years despite typical 3-year contracts, artificially inflating 2023 pre-tax profits by 60%
  • Capitalizes 44% of software development costs vs peer median of 29%, inflating earnings and operating cash flow by estimated $30M annually
  • Failed to report $546 million of customer funds in FY2022, representing material weakness in internal controls
  • Executive compensation tied to manipulated non-GAAP metrics including float revenue that was previously excluded after shareholder rejection
  • CEO received $120.5M compensation in 2021 while company lost $75M; shareholders rejected 2021 compensation plan
  • Heavy insider selling totaling ~$160M since 2021 led by CEO
  • Five top executives departed in 2023 including co-CEO and CFO
  • Less than 4% of large cap companies report material weaknesses in internal controls; Dayforce is one of only 10 S&P 500 companies with adverse auditor opinion
