Catalysts

  • Board refresh with three new directors bringing grocery and food distribution sector expertise
  • Sale-leaseback transaction of $950 million generating $780 million for share buybacks
  • Strategic review process comparing standalone operational improvements to strategic alternatives
  • Potential sale of grocery or distribution businesses separately or together to strategic buyers
  • Operational improvements: pricing strategy, private label expansion, supply chain optimization, vendor negotiations
  • Industry consolidation wave creating opportunities before window closes

Red Flags Alleged

  • Board failed to constructively engage with Macellum despite nominations in December 2021
  • Defensive board refresh in February 2022 without consulting Macellum or adding relevant expertise
  • Board misrepresented TSR performance by cherry-picking timeframes at stock's lowest points
  • Three extremely long-tenured directors (average 19 years) remain in key leadership positions
  • Four CEO changes in five years reflecting poor succession planning
  • New executives hired from Borden Dairy which filed bankruptcy in January 2020
  • Change of control provisions added December 2021 after Macellum nomination
  • CEO compensation increased 43% from 2014-2021 while EBIT declined 16%
  • Board members sold significant portions of holdings, own collectively just 0.87% of company
  • Failed acquisitions: Dan's, Caito/BRT, Martin's added $334mm cost but EBITDA declined
  • Dilutive Amazon warrant agreement: 4-15% dilution for minimal EBIT uplift
  • Company missed guidance six consecutive years prior to COVID
  • No detailed strategic plan or long-term goals disclosed since 2013 merger
