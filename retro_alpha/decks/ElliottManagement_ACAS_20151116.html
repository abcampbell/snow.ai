Thesis
American Capital trades at persistent 38% discount to NAV ($14.31 vs $19.72/share) due to ineffective management, poor capital allocation, unqualified board, and excessive overhead. Management's spin-out proposal permanently impairs value. Portfolio review, cost cuts, and strategic alternatives can unlock 24-63% upside.

Catalysts

  • Withdraw management's spin-out proposal
  • Add qualified independent board members with relevant experience
  • Portfolio review by experienced outside adviser and strategic monetization of broadly syndicated loan portfolio
  • Share repurchase program expansion
  • Cost cutting initiative targeting $50-75 million annual reduction
  • Strategic Review Committee to explore all options to maximize shareholder value

Red Flags Alleged

  • CEO erroneously overpaid leading to $10 million cash bonus in 2014
  • Multiple equity grants to CEO violated company incentive plans for several years before board became aware
  • Board lacked adequate controls to monitor executive compensation practices
  • Glass Lewis recommended voting against directors for 5 consecutive years
  • Compensation expense ratio among highest of publicly traded alternative asset managers
  • Overhead 56% higher than other internally managed BDCs
  • Board average tenure 15 years versus S&P 500 average of 8.4 years
  • Directors lack relevant investment experience despite overseeing investment firm
