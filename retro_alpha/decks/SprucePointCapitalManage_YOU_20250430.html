Catalysts

  • TSA Digital ID lane expansion displacing CLEAR's value proposition
  • Deteriorating Net Member Retention (fell from 95.3% in Q1 2022 to 81.4% in Q4 2024)
  • Delta exited all 8.3 million YOU shares by year end 2024
  • United eliminated free CLEAR benefit for top tier elite travelers
  • Deferred revenue added fell below recognized in 2024 for first time
  • Potential Amex partnership repricing or termination
  • TSA mandate requiring all travelers processed through CAT machines
  • IDEMIA expanding CAT-2 facial recognition to 400+ federalized airports
  • Airlines partnering with TSA on competing Touchless ID services

Red Flags Alleged

  • Chief Accounting Officer was Corporate Controller at Ubiquiti during period it inflated registered users by 85% (4 million claimed vs 609,000 actual)
  • CAO's CPA claim cannot be verified in CPAVerify.com database
  • Active member grace period extended from 45 to 51 days in mid-2024, then exact period no longer disclosed, potentially inflating member counts
  • Revenue recognition policy changed to no longer reduce revenue by estimated amounts due to credit card partner
  • Deferred revenue added ($115.8mm) fell below recognized ($145.1mm) in 2024 for first time since 2021
  • Customer Lifetime Value to CAC ratio plummeted from 22.0x (2021) to 10.0x (2024) after CFO said nothing would change it
  • R&D expense declined 1.3% in 2024 while technology team cut 20% (from 275 to 220)
  • Net Promoter Score dropped from 77 (2021) to 76 (2022) then stopped being disclosed; Comparably reports current NPS of -15
  • Cash distributed to related parties exceeded internal investment and acquisitions by $44 million over past three years
  • Enterprise value miscalculated by data providers, fails to capture $196 million tax receivable obligation
  • Changed refund policy to more restrictive terms potentially inflating cash flow
  • Gross margin potentially mischaracterized and inflated by 2,430 basis points
  • Stopped disclosing percentage of members verifying with TSA PreCheck after 2022 (was 56%)
  • Customer complaints of poor billing practices; TrustPilot rating 1.1 stars, BBB rating F
  • Former employee alleges CLEAR shares non-biometric customer data with service providers for marketing (favorite foods, credit card type, flight class)
  • Data breach risk from centralized database architecture criticized by identity experts as wrong approach
  • Customer data deletion requests took over two weeks and three emails to respond

Key Risks Acknowledged

  • Recession fears weighing on travel plans impacting subscriptions
  • CLEAR gets 64% of sign-ups from in-airport traffic vulnerable to travel slowdown
  • High borrow cost of shorted security
  • Potential for short squeeze
  • May close positions before price targets reached
