Red Flags Alleged

  • At least 70% of users are fake bots, likely 80%+ based on analysis of 54,065 unique users across 200+ paid K-12 classes
  • Fraudulent revenue at least equal to percentage of fraudulent users (70-80%+)
  • Bot networks operated using 'Group Control' software since 2015, controlling 500-1,000+ cell phones per operator
  • GSX operates its own bot farm with over 10,000 machines in a computer room
  • Third-party bot operators compensated through 2-5% commissions of would-be revenue, with GSX providing cash to legitimize transactions
  • Bot costs booked through sales/marketing expense or cost of sales to disguise fraud
  • Precise Joiners: 10.6% of users join at exact same second on multiple occasions across different weeks (5,742 users)
  • GSX IP Joiners: 28.2% of purported students share IP addresses with teachers/tutors despite no physical schools (15,239 users)
  • Burst Joiners: Large groups (20-30+ users) join simultaneously in coordinated bursts indicating automated bot activity (4,528 users)
  • Early Joiners: Users logging in implausibly early (30+ minutes before class) consistent with bot behavior (7,579 users)
  • Former GSX manager corroborated bot operation, explained Group Control software, named specific bot operator companies including Weishi and Baijia Youlian
  • Chairman Chen pledged $318 million (6 million Class B shares/9 million ADSs) creating margin call liquidation risk
