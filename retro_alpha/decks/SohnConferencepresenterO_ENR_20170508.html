Thesis
Energizer faces secular decline in alkaline batteries (3-4% annually), commoditization by private labels, retailer margin pressure, e-commerce disruption, and limited acquisition opportunities. Recent organic growth driven by temporary factors. Trading at 13x forward EV/EBITDA vs. 7x Duracell acquisition multiple.

Catalysts

  • Rising interest rates making yield companies less attractive
  • Continued secular decline in alkaline battery space
  • Disappointing earnings as temporary factors reverse
  • Poor acquisitions leading to debt or equity offerings

Red Flags Alleged

  • Channel stuffing: aggressive revenue recognition through inventory deloading and adding new displays in stores
  • Recent organic growth of 7% driven by temporary factors: 3% shelf space gains to be lapped, 3% incremental holiday activity
  • Q2 2017 showed inventory deload of -4% rendering organic growth flat
