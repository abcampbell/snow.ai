Thesis
SodaStream is not a fad but a beaten-down market leader with fixable problems trading at reasonable valuations. Western Europe business and CO2 refill franchise alone each worth nearly entire market cap. High barriers to entry in CO2 distribution network create sustainable moat.

Catalysts

  • Resumption of high growth and margins after Q4 inventory overhang normalizes
  • Samsung refrigerator partnership rollout across Europe and additional models launching
  • New flavor partnerships (Ocean Spray, Welch's, Crystal Light, Kool Aid, etc.) hitting shelves
  • Channel inventory right-sizing in 1H 2014 leading to margin normalization
  • Potential acquisition or large partner deal similar to GMCR/Coca-Cola
  • Shift to higher-margin consumables business over time

Key Risks Acknowledged

  • Big inventory overhang from Q4, so Q1 estimates probably need to come down and earnings could be ugly
  • Inventory overhang could last beyond Q1, meaning gross margins might take longer to recover
  • Recent weak results could prove not temporary and trends of slowing growth and falling margins could continue
  • Any slowdown in sales of CO2 refills would hurt profitability and could indicate increased churn among active users
  • Competitors could make inroads
  • Valuation multiples, which aren't low, could compress further
  • SodaStream may have already penetrated most of the home carbonation market thus revenue growth will continue to decline
