Catalysts

  • May production figures and 2Q results showing poor all-in unit economics post-halving
  • Potential regulatory changes to Texas grid incentive programs in 2025 legislative session
  • Corsicana facility development risks and local political opposition
  • Shareholders voting on doubling authorized common shares at annual meeting

Red Flags Alleged

  • Serial dilution: issued $507m in stock (18% dilution) through April 2024 at prices in line with current stock price
  • Excessive management compensation: CEO and Chairman each received $51m in stock awards over 3 years plus $1m salary
  • Never produced positive cash flow or decent return despite operations
  • Bitcoin production per share and bitcoin holdings per share steadily declining
  • Substandard operations at Rockdale: shoddy infrastructure, pipes burst in December 2022 due to operational incompetence affecting 25% of capacity
  • Regulatory arbitrage: participating in demand response programs not as intended due to inability to fully curtail during extreme heat
  • Navarro County Commissioners unanimously rejected tax abatement for Corsicana in March 2024
  • Former mining technician described operations as 'harrowing' and 'ramshackle' with health and safety violations
  • Two ongoing legal disputes with former hosting customers alleging substandard operating conditions
  • All-in cost to mine bitcoin ($142k per coin) far exceeds direct mining costs touted by management

Key Risks Acknowledged

  • Bitcoin price movements could drive share price temporarily higher
  • Company has $700m+ cash on hand and bitcoin holdings
