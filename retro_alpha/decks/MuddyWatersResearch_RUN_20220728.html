Thesis
Muddy Waters is short Sunrun (RUN), viewing it as an uneconomic business built on exaggerated subscriber values, abusive tax incentive practices including inflated ITC bases, and ABS potentially exposed to bankruptcy. The company uses aggressive modeling assumptions divorced from reality to create equity story and manipulate tax benefits, facing potential IRS claw back of $948 million.
