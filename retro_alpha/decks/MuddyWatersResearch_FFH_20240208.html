Red Flags Alleged

  • Consistently manipulated asset values and income through accounting gains from value destructive transactions
  • Allied World acquisition in 2017 caused insurance profitability to lag, pressuring aggressive accounting lever-pulling starting 2018
  • Recipe mis-marking: carried equity at premium to market price 2015-2021, took private at 53% premium to avoid $251 million losses
  • Quess de-consolidation in 2018 created $889.9 million artificial gain; repeatedly changed accounting methods to avoid impairment
  • EXCO Resources carried well above market value; fair value 3x market value as of Q3 2023, overstating book value by $245 million
  • Grivalia Properties: swapped shares with Eurobank to create paper gains of $171 million in circular transactions
  • RiverStone JV used off-balance sheet debt (AVLNs) to create illusory $117.1 million gain and avoid recognizing losses on seven associates
  • Fairfax Africa: spent cash on accounting gimmicks to de-consolidate loss-making subsidiary, understated losses by $105.5 million
  • APR Energy: paid friend (Seaspan) to acquire APR to avoid booking $109 million losses, later compensated Seaspan for overpayment
  • Eurolife OMERS transaction disguised debt as equity investment, created unjustified fair value gains of $261.8 million
  • Odyssey and Brit transactions in 2021 were disguised financing that resulted in highly dubious accounting gains of $544.5 million
  • GoDigit stock promotion using TRSes: improperly recognized gains inconsistent with valuation changes, claimed profitability when company showed net losses
  • IFRS 17 adoption: significant outlier among P&C insurers in financial impacts, 'too good to be true'
  • Farmers Edge under-impaired by ~$66 million when market cap crashed to ~C$10 million, undisclosed additional loans of C$75 million
  • Gulf Insurance: bought out remaining stake at rich 2.4x book value multiple, taking ~$300 million gain in December 2023
  • CEO Prem Watsa potential conflict: purchased Atlas shares alongside Fairfax investment, later sold to Fairfax ahead of minorities
