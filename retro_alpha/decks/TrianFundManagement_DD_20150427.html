Catalysts

  • Four Trian nominees elected to Board to drive oversight and accountability
  • Assessment of corporate structure to determine optimal portfolio configuration
  • Elimination of $2-4 billion in excess corporate costs
  • Improved capital allocation including M&A review and increased dividends
  • Enhanced corporate governance including compensation alignment with performance
  • Spin-off of Performance Chemicals (Chemours) already announced

Red Flags Alleged

  • DuPont sold Performance Coatings (Axalta) in 2013 for ~$4bn, transferring over $6bn of shareholder wealth to private equity; Axalta EBITDA margins improved from 8% in 2011 to 20% in 2014 after sale
  • Management released 9 different versions of 2011 EPS figures
  • Danisco operating margins have fallen 33% since acquired by DuPont in 2011
  • 63% of new products introduced in last 5 years cannibalized existing product sales (2006 disclosure); company ceased reporting cannibalization in 2007
  • DuPont stock price has declined aggregate $12 on trading day following all 32 earnings/guidance announcements since April 2009
  • Chemours being spun off with poor corporate governance: staggered board until at least 2017, 80% supermajority vote required to amend bylaws, 5-year non-compete restrictions
  • CEO sold over half her stock since Trian's investment
  • Fresh Start cost savings of $925mm announced but no commitment to flow savings to bottom line
  • Management used only 25% of Performance Coatings sale proceeds to offset dilution; paid ~$1bn in tax leakage on sale
  • Rynaxypyr insecticide generated only 3% of 2014 revenue despite being cited as breakthrough innovation; crop chemicals revenue growth trailed peers by 240 bps annually 2008-2014
  • Sorona biomaterials business accounted for less than 1% of 2013 sales and believed unprofitable
