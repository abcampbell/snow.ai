Catalysts

  • $255m in bond interest payments due in 4Q23, particularly $168m on November 1st on 2030 notes
  • Dwindling cash and liquidity as year progresses
  • Potential equity offering to raise capital
  • Ongoing standoff with concentrated bondholder group owning 90% of bonds
  • Seasonally slowest period in 4Q with over $250m interest payments

Red Flags Alleged

  • Pre-announced better than expected 2Q23 EBITDA primarily due to large one-time loan sales, reeks of pumping shares ahead of potential equity offering
  • NPS (Net Promoter Score) claims are smoke and mirrors - asking customers right after delivery, not 45 days later when registration and inspection issues surface
  • Fundamentally flawed data analytics - too slow, overly reliant on historical data, not equipped to capture macro changes
  • Management lacks seasoned automotive and operational experience
  • Dual-class share structure with Garcias controlling 88% voting power
  • Failed to disclose average age of vehicles despite metric's importance
  • Misleading portrayal of liquidity - most of $1.5bn touted availability is restricted to inventory/loan financing, not available for debt service
