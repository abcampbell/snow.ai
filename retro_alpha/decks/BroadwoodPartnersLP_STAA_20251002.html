Thesis
STAAR Surgical stockholders should reject Alcon's $28/share acquisition offer. The deal undervalues STAAR during temporary challenges that are abating. STAAR's leading ICL technology, strong fundamentals, growing markets, and management projections support significantly higher standalone value. The rushed, flawed process with conflicts favored Alcon over stockholders.

Catalysts

  • China inventory normalization in second half of 2025
  • Launch of EVO+ lens product
  • Management cost reduction initiatives ($25M+ SG&A savings)
  • Return to growth and profitability in 2026
  • Continued ICL market share gains from LASIK procedures
  • Growing myopia epidemic (1.1 billion candidates)
  • New Swiss manufacturing facility reaching capacity
  • Surgeon education and AI adoption for ICL procedures

Red Flags Alleged

  • Board conducted no proactive outreach to alternative buyers despite logical strategic suitors
  • Management projections abruptly revised downward within 10 days to justify lower valuation
  • Citi used excessive 14% discount rate versus 8.1% actual WACC to lower DCF valuation
  • Rushed 30-day sale process driven by Alcon's desire to sign before Q2 2025 results disclosed
  • Board Chair Dr. Yeu received hundreds of thousands from Alcon over 7 years as paid consultant
  • Dr. Yeu served on Alcon speaker's bureau promoting their products, conflict not disclosed to directors until 2 days before signing
  • Former CEO Tom Frinzi has close relationship with Alcon CEO as golf partners
  • Gibson Dunn served as STAAR counsel in June 2024 then represented Alcon in acquisition
  • Citi's $27M contingent fee represents 16% of its 2025 public sell-side M&A fees
  • CEO stands to receive $24M for 5 months of work with single-trigger equity vesting
  • NEOs receive tax gross-ups and 160% of target performance assumption without justification
  • Two interested parties contacted Board but not provided diligence until hours before signing
  • Window shop provision provided Alcon preferential matching rights

Key Risks Acknowledged

  • Elevated inventory levels in China distributors (now resolved)
  • Weakened consumer confidence and spending in China
  • Softened macroeconomic environment impacting refractive procedures
  • Near-term revenue decline in largest market (China)
  • Tariff risks and trade war concerns
  • Competitive landscape evolving in China market
