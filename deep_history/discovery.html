§2 — VOLCANIC FORCING: EVERY COMMODITY TESTED

Superposed epoch analysis: what happens to 17 economic series after VEI≥5 eruptions? Plus Tambora 1815 and Laki 1783 case studies.

Honest Result
Directionally correct but NOT statistically significant

Only 12 VEI≥5 eruptions overlap with most economic series — power is low. Point estimates: Sound Toll +32.9%, Antwerp wheat +27.1%, Paris wheat +12.1%, conflict +11.3%. All directionally consistent with volcanic disruption but p > 0.05 for every series. Interest rates drop 9.7% post-eruption — possible flight to safety. Nile shows slight suppression. Wine harvest barely moves. The earlier cross-correlation analysis (r = 0.40 at lag 0) used a different volcanic proxy and longer overlap — the signal depends on how you measure volcanic forcing.

SYNTHESIS

The dominant signal in 4,600 years of financial data is climate forcing through agriculture. Solar minima → cooling → harvest failure → grain price spike → real wage collapse → political instability → war. The chain takes 5-15 years to complete. It works across civilizations — Nile floods predict Chinese droughts, volcanic eruptions spike grain prices on both sides of Eurasia.

The Great Divergence was gradual (post-1600, not sudden), entangled with Atlantic trade and New World silver. The Price Revolution hammered real wages by 45%. Interest rates show a 4,400-year secular decline that halves every 700 years.

The Fourth Turning? r = 0.021. The real cycles are solar (~11yr, ~60yr, ~200yr), not generational (~80yr).
