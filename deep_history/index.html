IX · THE FOURTH TURNING — TESTED

Strauss-Howe ~80-year generational cycle (1435–2030) overlaid against independently-compiled crisis intensity (216 wars, panics, pandemics, revolutions, 1400–2025). Does the theory hold up when you test it against data it wasn't fitted to?

VERDICT: r = 0.021

The Strauss-Howe 80-year cycle has zero correlation with independently-measured crisis intensity.

The only signal — war clustering in "Crisis" turnings (79% vs 62%) — is circular: they drew the boxes around the wars they already knew about.

Exogenous physical series (Nile floods, Chinese disasters, sunspots) show zero phase-dependence, confirming no hidden common driver.

The "Awakening" phase is actually the most crisis-intensive (mean 18.3 vs 16.4 for "Crisis"), and the t-test is not significant (p = 0.15). It's a narrative framework, not a quantitative one.
