Two weaknesses this table makes visible. The “scored 1” column has no written definition — it is a residual, meaning whatever matched neither guide, so a hedge and a non-answer land in the same bucket. And the highlighted rows are questions where not one of the 24 models took the state position or refused: they cannot distinguish anything. The two control rows are supposed to behave that way; the COVID rows are not, and were included on a prediction that did not hold.
