Loss channel. Haircut applied to fair-value + amortised-cost French sovereign securities + public-sector loans, pre-tax (a loss-making bank cannot recognise the deferred tax). The CDC toggle adds the Livret A/LDDS/LEP balances centralised at the Caisse des Dépôts, a state-guaranteed claim that the EBA books as central-government exposure (LBP ≈ €65bn, BPCE ≈ €100bn est., Crédit Mutuel ≈ €25bn est.). The insurer toggle adds the group life insurer's French sovereign book at an assumed shareholder pass-through after policyholder participation (CNP €54bn for LBP; CAA €51bn for Crédit Agricole; Cardif, Sogecap and BPCE Assurances not disclosed, set to 0).

Spread channel (table only). ΔCET1 = −D × fair-value exposure × Δspread × (1 − tax). Amortised-cost holdings are not marked; rate risk is swap-hedged, spread vs Bund is not, so OAT–Bund is the right shock variable.

Thresholds. MDA = CET1 SREP requirement incl. buffers (dividend/coupon restrictions). TSCR = Pillar 1 4.5% + CET1 share of P2R (binding minimum; breach ⇒ failing-or-likely-to-fail territory).
