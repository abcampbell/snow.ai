Facts

What drives the scores

The raw inputs behind the grades, colored by how good each one is on its own: green is favorable, red unfavorable. Land cost is FHFA residential lots inside a metro and county lots discounted by 75% more than 30 miles from one, so treat it as relative.

  • Built from one pull of each public source (see the Region Engine method). Climate is the last full calendar year, not a 30-year normal.
  • Each stop is a 25-mile circle around the town center, not the whole state. Rural land between stops is often far cheaper than the circle's median.
  • Screening only: interconnection queues, water rights, zoning, incentives, and fiber need direct confirmation.
