The Drive

Dollywood to Ely: 27 stops across Tennessee, Missouri, Nebraska, Iowa, South Dakota, Wyoming, Montana, Idaho, and Nevada, in the order driven. Each stop is scored by the Region Engine within 25 miles for nine land uses and 27 factors. Star the places you liked, set your own weights, and the grid ranks everything your way. The engine also learns which factors your stars line up with.

Rank

Rank them your way

Three ways to rank, all feeding the grid below. Your stars: rate each stop 1–5 from having been there. My score: set a weight on any factor. Learned: the engine compares your stars with the factor scores and builds a weighting from whatever your stars correlate with.

  • Built from one pull of each public source (see the Region Engine method). Climate is the last full calendar year, not a 30-year normal.
  • Each stop is a 25-mile circle around the town center. Land cost is FHFA residential lots inside a metro, and county lots otherwise (discounted 75% more than 30 miles from a metro, or the raw-land band in the Great Basin), so treat it as relative. Carey and Craters of the Moon read high because the nearest county record is Blaine County (Sun Valley).
  • "Learned" is a correlation across at most 27 places: a pointer to what you value, not a fitted model.
