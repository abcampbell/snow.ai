04 · The machine

One loop, three scales

Source below retail, verify, underwrite, transform, distribute, and recycle the proceeds. The stages stay the same from a single 40-acre parcel to a whole valley; only the data feeding them changes.

SCALE 1 · PROVEN

Single asset

One house or parcel taken through sourcing, verification, valuation, negotiation, financing, and close. Plays: seller-financed notes, raw-to-improved flips, curated resale.

SCALE 2

Block and corridor

Multi-family, commercial, office, and industrial. The same tools aimed at assemblage: ownership mapping across a block, permit velocity as a leading indicator, anchor analysis, and FAR arbitrage.

SCALE 3

Region and town

100 to 10,000 acres. Grid, water, labor, and land cost decide the use before any parcel is priced: east of the 100th meridian the binding constraint is usually power and zoning, west of it water. Overlays: mineral patents, solar adjacency, conservation easements. Region engine →

05 · Systems

Twelve systems, each a repeatable decision

The intellectual property is the decision method; the code is just how it currently runs. Each system works at any scale and in any region. Screen a parcel above and each card shows its 1–10 score for that place (log scale, 5 = middle of the road).

  • Parcel ownership, seller basis, and permits are not live yet. County assessor and permit systems vary by jurisdiction and are often not public APIs, so the report links out to them. Per-county adapters are on the roadmap, and building them is part of the moat.
  • The Nevada well-log pack covers the basins downloaded so far; elsewhere, and where it is silent, groundwater comes from USGS monitoring wells, some measured decades ago.
  • There is no national traffic-count service, so traffic exposure is a proxy built from road class and distance, not measured daily traffic.
  • FHFA land values are for single-family residential lots, so raw acreage outside town trades far lower. In the Great Basin plug-in, rural points use the raw-land price band instead.
  • Zillow, Redfin, and homes.com block automated reading of their pages, so listing links are resolved from the address in the URL.
  • A screen is not a survey, title search, or appraisal. Every GO still needs a field walk.
