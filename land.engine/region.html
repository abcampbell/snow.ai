Region Engine

Type a place and an idea in plain English, like "buy southern Idaho and turn it into data centers". The engine detects the use, samples the area, and pulls power lines, generation, gas, hazards, water, climate, terrain, land ownership, land cost, labor, and access from public sources. It then scores the area for that use, so you can switch the use and rescore instantly or rank several areas side by side.

Method

How a region gets its grade

Every factor is scored 0–100 from its raw value, and each use weights the factors differently; the grade is the weighted average. A data center leans on grid access, power price, and cooling; a homestead leans on water, land cost, and climate. Switch the use to see the same data scored differently.
