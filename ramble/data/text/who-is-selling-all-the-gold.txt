Last week I mentioned I had a toe back in the water in gold, despite the ‘chart looking like death.’
Well, sometimes the chart looks like crap because it’s about to collapse.

Then again, sometimes the reason you put a toe in the water is so you feel comfortable diving in when things start looking terrible. So today we’re going to try to answer three questions:
1. Who is puking gold, and why?
2. What’s an interesting way to add to our gold position, so as to bring portfolio vol up a bit?
3. Is there anything negatively correlated to gold we could lever up alongside it to help balance the book?
Let’s start by process of elimination, rather than throwing up our hands and tossing certain venerable Old World banking dynasties under the bus, shall we?
