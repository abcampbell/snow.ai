The core mechanism this ledger documents: 73.9% of world mine silver is byproduct of lead/zinc, copper and gold mines (WSS 2026) — the silver price cannot ration most of its own supply. Indium is 100% a zinc-smelter byproduct. Tungsten and rare-earth supply is mine-diversified but chokepointed at conversion (APT ~China; separation ~90% China, heavy REE ~99%). Stockpiles: the West publishes inventories to the kilogram; China's SRB and Russia's Rosreserv publish nothing — opacity is a data point.

HONEST NEGATIVES

· Three delegate workers hit iteration caps before writing files; outputs were salvaged from their research summaries — every point retained its quote+URL, but they carry one fewer verification pass than the cost-curve layer.
· Indium: NO producer anywhere (including OECD) publicly reports facility-level output — points are USGS country estimates; Korea Zinc's #1 status is asserted by trade press, not company disclosure.
· China/Russia state stockpiles are structurally opaque — SRB entries are episodic purchase reports (SMM/Reuters), not inventories. Absence of an entry ≠ absence of a stockpile.
· REE mine points mix TREO and NdPr-oxide bases — the basis field makes each point self-describing; do not sum across bases.
· Myanmar 31 kt REO is a pre-disruption 2024 figure; flow was running ~−89% y/y by Feb 2025. China's 2025+ REE quotas are no longer published (state secret since 2024) — 270 kt is the last public anchor.
· Stocks (vaults, ETPs, government inventories) and flows (t/yr production) share the tonnes unit but are different objects — the basis field tags which is which; never net them.
