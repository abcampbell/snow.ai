Methods & Record

Every claim on this site traces to an artifact in the project repository. This page states the rules the record is kept under, and the results that argue against us.

The headline, exactly

Competition declines before complexity peaks in 24 of 25 blind-graded polities with an identified peak (binomial p≈7.8×10−7 against a fair coin). An earlier count of 26/27 included two polities whose records are ongoing (right-censored); they are excluded from the primary count. If a further terminal-edge case (France) is also excluded: 23/24. The statement is an ordering (“declines before”), not a causal claim.

Blind grading protocol

Scores were produced by graders given only a theory-free definitions file and a bare decade grid per polity — no project files, no hypotheses, no sight of other graders’ work. Cause-side (S) and outcome-side (C) variables were scored by different graders. Integration was re-scored under a mechanism-only rubric (machinery-in-motion verbs, never prosperity nouns) after an early version was found to correlate 0.86 with complexity — the re-score cut that to 0.53 median with zero polities above 0.9. A follow-up regrade by fresh graders reproduced the curves at median rank-correlation 0.72–0.85 per variable.

Record policy

Preregistered and pre-rule verdicts are never retroactively changed. Where a later, better-defined unit (e.g. a dynasty split) yields a different verdict, the new verdict is reported beside the record, never over it. Joseon’s preregistered outcome (+180, outside the predicted window) stays on the books as written.

Unit-definition (split) rule

Whether a long polity is one unit or two is decided by a shape rule (55% drawdown / 3-point / 5-point thresholds) authored blind on 49 anonymized complexity curves before any names or verdicts were attached. The rule splits 5 of 49 series; it keeps Rome whole. Splits driven by dynasty identity alone are disclosed as such.

Living polities

USA, Britain, Russia, Prussia, Habsburg Austria: records ongoing (right-censored at the 1914 cliff or the present). They carry banners on their pages and are excluded from every peak-aligned statistic on this site.

Scope

One instrument (one model family graded all polities), one source tradition (anglophone historiography), blind-graded internally, externally untested at available resolution. Complexity here is record-producing complexity: the framework’s domain is polities legible to their own archives. Ordering ≠ causation.
