Case Studies

Texas Negative Prices ERCOT

Wind overgeneration + inflexible baseload → negative clearing prices. Energy-only market (no capacity payments) amplifies volatility. Price cap: $5,000/MWh during scarcity.

Homer City Redevelopment PJM

Retired coal plant (heat rate ~10) being converted to gas. Economics: reuse existing grid interconnection point rather than wait 5+ years in queue. Capacity auction provides revenue certainty.

California Evening Prices CAISO

Duck curve: solar depression midday, evening ramp requires gas peakers at higher heat rates. NP-15 vs SP-15 split via Path 15 transmission constraint.

Nuclear Pipeline Multi-region

MSFT: Three Mile Island 837 MW restart. AMZN: Susquehanna 1.92 GW + X-energy SMR. GOOGL: Kairos Power SMR. META: 6.6 GW across Vistra, Oklo, TerraPower.
