# Math to Atoms v036 — resources and healthy lives in dollars

Adds a shared, editable valuation to the summary and full model and incorporates
the energy-GDP research into essay v008 (15 November 2026). The existing graph,
scores, worked paths and historical/forecast plots remain available.

At 40% improvement where used, over 2026–2046:

| Reach / use | Resources/yr | Health/yr | Combined/yr |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 25% / 50% | $1.81T | $2.03T | $3.84T |
| 100% / 50% | $7.24T | $8.11T | $15.4T |
| 100% / 100% | $14.5T | $16.2T | $30.7T |

Health uses an explicitly chosen $14,500/DALY averted: health itself, excluding
another count of medical spending or earnings. Baseline burden is the existing
2.797 billion rounded-group sum (displayed 2.80 billion); its whole-burden value
is $40.6T/yr. That is not a claim all burden is mathematically avoidable.
Franklin et al. (2023) explain GDP/person valuation benchmarks and their limits.

Only resources and health are added. Energy output uses a conditional 1.5
capital-response factor following the IMF Chile framework (Guo 2024); it is
an alternative expression of the same energy benefit. Climate ($100/tCO2) and
delivery ($10T annual project spending, even construction spending, 5% capital
charge) are separately valued and excluded because overlaps are unresolved.

All values are gross before implementation costs, with fixed references and
even phase-in. Totals are neither extra GDP nor cash income. Cumulative totals
are undiscounted. No empirical attribution from solving every frontier theorem
to the selected 40% physical improvement has been established.

The summary shares four slider settings and five dollar assumptions through
validated URL parameters, without editing full-model storage. The model stores
valuation assumptions with the workspace; Undo restores them. JSON export
schema 20 accepts earlier formats, including 18/19 with valuation defaults.

Validation: shared calculation tests; all three cases and price sensitivity;
invalid inputs and missing spending; URL/Undo/export migration; retained graph,
worked paths, plot endpoints; summary/model/essay at mobile and desktop widths.
The Word draft retains 13 figures, adds a valuation table and two research
sources, and was rendered for visual review. Only approved versioned assets and
entry points are published; local render output is excluded.
