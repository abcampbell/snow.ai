# Math to Atoms v035

Fix chart geometry and make the GDP boundary explicit. The default, 50-year and 100-year live v034 forecasts were traced: their data paths already ended at the selected year. Presentation was misleading because outer columns had wider plot content than middle columns and phone CSS assigned aspect ratios different from the SVG viewBoxes. The upper histories and lower gain plots therefore had different visible right edges and row baselines.

v035 uses equal column gaps/padding and the intrinsic SVG aspect ratios on both summary and model. Every top chart and lower gain plot carries the selected end year and a vertical guide at the actual rightmost forecast coordinate. History ends at the last source observation; extrapolated trends and assumed gains continue to the chosen date. End dates are emphasized. The 100% shorter-time case explicitly discloses that the output/hour conversion has no finite endpoint, rather than pretending an infinite productivity value is drawable.

Rename the economic headline Gross savings. The historical GDP chart is labeled as context; the comparison names its assumed $120T reference, and additional GDP is explicitly unestimated. The summary and detailed model now show the same live component table: default energy $12T → $600B/year; other production $24T → $1.2T/year; disaster losses $224B → $11.2B/year; total $1.8112T/year, shown to three significant digits.

The explanation states that bases are assumptions, shared supplier costs still need checking, and implementation costs have not been deducted. A 20% energy-price example gives $2.4T/year of bill savings on the default $12T base, equal to 2% of the $120T reference; it is not labeled additional GDP or annual GDP growth. Missing channels are real resources versus price transfers, subsequent production/new capabilities, transition costs and changes in demand. Sources checked November 14, 2026: BEA price/quantity GDP FAQ, BEA industry value-added guide, and IEA Economic growth. No multiplier or causal GDP forecast is added.

All scenario arithmetic, reference data, three-significant-digit formatting, shared four-slider URL format, saved state, graph scoring and worked examples remain. v035 full model imports the updated shared timeline renderer. Cover GDP explanation changes with the existing sliders and is also server-rendered for the no-JS/data-load fallback.
