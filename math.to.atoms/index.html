The assumptions

Your log scale: each point multiplies by 3×. Today’s math and capability = 3.

How scoring works

Inputs stay on the 1–10 log scale. The computed ladder can extend beyond it. A ladder value of 3 means an index of 1.

w(s) = b^(s − 3)Problem = w(I)^α / (w(M)^λ × w(C)^κ) Math = [Σ (link/3) × w(I) × w(C)^−κ]^α / w(D)^λ Outcome = Σ (link/3) × problem scoreLadder = 3 + log_b(index)

I = impact; M = math understanding required; C = physical capability required; D = difficulty of the math.

Math and outcome scores add contributions, so breadth earns points. Math difficulty is applied once to the field. The formulas are unchanged from v006.

Capability drag is a proxy for engineering and validation barriers. Feedback latency is not measured separately.
