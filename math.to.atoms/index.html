What is better math worth?

Better mathematics could lower costs and save healthy years. The question is how much we can turn into working technology.

What is assumed?

The $1.81T includes energy, other production costs and avoided disaster losses. Health stays separate. Change the assumptions in the model →

The takeaway

The largest possible payoffs can need years of engineering and testing. Some nearer-term opportunities sit in maintenance, logistics and risk prediction. The useful question is which mathematical advances can change something we can actually build or use.

The numbers above are one scenario, not a price for all mathematics or a GDP forecast. The graph ranks research priorities; the benefit estimates use separate assumptions.
