II · WHERE THE INFORMATION LIVES — block-masking

Hide an entire complex ("all commodities") and reconstruct every member from the rest of the cross-section. Removes within-block interpolation — what remains is genuine cross-block linkage. Overall: 83.2% (vs 90.9% single-mask — harder by design). Training explicitly on blocks adds nothing (83.3%): the single-mask model had already learned the cross-block structure.
The irreducible residual lives in commodities and FX — a masked gold or oil move often simply isn't present in the rest of the cross-section to be recovered. Direct evidence the ceiling is data-limited.

III · THE HONEST NEGATIVE — forward correlation

The completion-implied correlation readout does not beat the frozen forward-63d bar (z-MAE 0.2426 vs bar 0.2172 — ~11% worse; trailing persistence is near-optimal for slow structure). What is defensible: the completer reconstructs the contemporaneous 9×9 matrix at z-MAE 0.0783 — faithful conditional cross-section reconstruction, which is what the scenario and linkage boards actually need. Framed as such, not dressed up as a forward win.
